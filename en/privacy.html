Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

In short: we keep what Bumi needs to work (your goals, your progress and your conversations with Bumi). We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps. You can delete everything at any time in the app, under Profile › Delete my account.

1. Who is responsible

Bumi is an app developed by Santiago Paz, an independent developer based in Argentina ("we", "us"). For any question about your data, write to bumiapp2026@gmail.com.

2. What data we keep

  • Your account. When you open Bumi, a guest account is created with an anonymous identifier. If you choose to save it with Apple or Google, that service gives us an identifier and your email (if you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we get the relay address, not your real one) and, if you share it, your name.
  • What you tell us at the start. Your answers in the first-run flow: what you'd like us to call you, your goals, how much time you have each day and anything you share about previous attempts.
  • Your plan and progress. Goals, pillars, tasks, completed missions, streaks, XP, coins, shop items, challenges, and the name and look you gave Bumi.
  • Your conversations with Bumi. The messages you type or dictate in the chat and Bumi's replies.
  • Reports you make. If you tap «Report» on one of Bumi's replies, we keep that reply, your previous message and the reason, so we can review them and improve.
  • Minimal technical data. Your language, your time zone (to know when your day starts and ends), the date of your last visit, and a log of each AI use (which feature, how much text was processed and its cost) so we can keep usage limits.
  • On your phone, not on our servers: your sound and language preferences and the time of your daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't access your location, contacts or photos.
  • We never see or store your card details: payments are processed by Apple (or Google, on Android).

4. What we use it for

  • To build your plan, show you what to do each day and keep track of your progress.
  • So Bumi can reply in the chat and remember what matters to you.
  • So you don't lose your progress if you change phones (if you saved your account).
  • To manage your subscription and keep the app working and secure.

The legal basis is providing the service you asked for by using the app and, where applicable, your consent.

5. Artificial intelligence

To build your plan and reply in the chat, Bumi sends what's needed from what you wrote (your goals, your answers, your messages and the context of your progress) to OpenRouter, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude model (Anthropic). They process it to generate the reply, under their own policies. We don't send them your email.

Bumi's replies are generated by AI: they can be wrong and are not a substitute for advice from a health, mental-health or financial professional.

6. The microphone

If you use the microphone to dictate in the chat, your voice is turned into text by the system's own speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which may process it on their servers. Bumi only receives the resulting text. We don't record or store your voice. The microphone is only used when you tap it and give permission.

7. Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need for the app to work, and only for that:

  • Supabase: hosts the database and accounts (servers in the United States).
  • OpenRouter and Anthropic: artificial intelligence (see section 5).
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, subscription billing and speech recognition.

Because these servers are outside your country, your data is transferred internationally. We only do this with providers that offer appropriate safeguards. We may also share data if a law or a competent authority requires it.

8. How long we keep it

For as long as you have your account. When you delete it in the app (Profile › Delete my account), your account and everything linked to it is deleted immediately and permanently: Bumi, your goals, missions, streaks, coins, conversations, the AI usage log and your reports. Our provider's automatic backups may keep that data for a limited time until they roll over.

If you delete the app from your phone without deleting your account, the data stays on our servers. If you were a guest and can no longer get in, write to us and we'll help you delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to bumiapp2026@gmail.com. We reply within the time limits set by law. In Argentina, the Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP) is the supervisory authority under Personal Data Protection Law 25,326, and you can file complaints with it. If you live elsewhere (for example in the European Union or Brazil), you also have the rights your local law gives you.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfect, but we look after your data as if it were ours.

11. Children

Bumi is not meant for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you're a parent or guardian and think a child gave us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, if the change is significant, let you know in the app.
